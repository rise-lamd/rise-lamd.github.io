### Frameworks & Open Source

The laboratory develops and maintains open-source toolkits resulting from our research:

* **[pyUDLF / UDLF](https://github.com/UDLF):** Python framework for unsupervised distance learning and multimedia re-ranking (ACM Multimedia 2023).
* **[GRaCE](https://github.com/thcastilho/interpretable-embeddings):** Interpretable graph- and rank-based contextual embeddings for textual and multimedia data (IJCNN 2025).
* **[MARETopic](https://github.com/thcastilho/maretopic):** Training-free, interpretable topic modeling anchored on prototype leader documents via manifold learning (EMNLP 2026).
* **[SGCC](https://github.com/lopes-leonardo/sgcc):** Self-Supervised Graph Convolutional Clustering.
* **[USRF](https://github.com/UDLF/USRF/):** Unsupervised Selective Rank Fusion Framework.
* **[WSEF](https://github.com/UDLF/WSEF/):** Weakly-Supervised Experiments Framework.
