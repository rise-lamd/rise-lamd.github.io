The **RISE-LAMD Laboratory** conducts Research, Development, and Innovation (R&D&I) in **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, and **Information Retrieval**. Our research focuses on recognition, inference, and search over language and multimedia data.

We operate across multiple frontiers, including natural language processing, computer vision, and textual and multimedia information retrieval. We design innovative solutions to advance the state of the art in our fields, converting unstructured multimodal data (text, images, audio, video, and signals) into structured knowledge for decision support.
