The **RISE-LAMD** laboratory at UNESP (DEMAC/IGCE — Rio Claro Campus) is pleased to announce open recruitment for funded research fellowships associated with the international research project **RATIONAL: Robust Augmented Retrieval for Natural Language Inference over Transformer-based Models**, co-funded by **FAPESP** (São Paulo Research Foundation, Brazil) and the **SNSF** (Swiss National Science Foundation, Switzerland).

The project is an international partnership between the **Idiap Research Institute** (Switzerland) and **UNESP** (Brazil), co-coordinated by **Prof. Dr. Andre Freitas (Idiap)** and **Prof. Dr. Daniel C. G. Pedronette (UNESP)**.

The core research objective is to investigate and develop robust evidence-based methods, retrieved through advanced Information Retrieval techniques, for natural language inference tasks powered by Large Language Models (LLMs).

---

### Available Fellowships

#### 1. Postdoctoral Fellowship (PD)
- **Openings:** 1 position
- **Application Deadline:** September 20, 2026
- **Planned Start:** October 2026
- **Duration:** 24 months (with possibility of extension)
- **Stipend:** FAPESP standard rates (BRL 12,570.00/month tax-free + 10% Research Overhead / Reserva Técnica)
- **Location:** UNESP — Rio Claro/SP, Brazil (DEMAC / IGCE)
- **Profile:** PhD in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related fields with solid experience in NLP, LLMs, or Information Retrieval.

#### 2. Technical Training Fellowship (TT-4 FAPESP 40h)
- **Openings:** 2 positions
- **Application Deadline:** September 20, 2026
- **Planned Start:** October 2026
- **Duration:** 24 months
- **Stipend:** FAPESP standard rates (BRL 4,520.00/month tax-free, 40h/week)
- **FAPESP Requirements:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (or related fields) with two years of post-graduation research/professional experience, **or** a Master's degree (MSc).
- **Location:** UNESP — Rio Claro/SP, Brazil (DEMAC / IGCE)
- **Activities:** Direct interaction and collaborative development with the project research team (faculty, researchers, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows).
