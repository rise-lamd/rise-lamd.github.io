---
title: "RISE-LAMD Laboratory is formally established at UNESP"
date: 2025-09-29
summary: "The RISE-LAMD Laboratory was formally established at UNESP by the Ordinance of September 29, 2025, based at DEMAC/IGCE and UNESPetro."
---

We are pleased to announce that the **RISE-LAMD Laboratory** (*Recognition, Inference, and SEarch on Language And Multimedia Data*) has been formally established at **UNESP — Universidade Estadual Paulista** by the Ordinance of September 29, 2025.

Headquartered at the Department of Statistics, Applied Mathematics and Computing (DEMAC) within the Institute of Geosciences and Exact Sciences (IGCE), Rio Claro Campus, and working in close partnership with UNESPetro (Center for Geosciences Applied to Petroleum), the laboratory consolidates an established record of scientific excellence in Research, Development, and Innovation (R&D).

Our mission is to advance state-of-the-art research in **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, and **Information Retrieval**, creating both robust theoretical foundations and practical applications to transform complex, heterogeneous data (text, images, signals, time series, and multimodal models) into structured knowledge for decision support.
