RISE-LAMD is a research group at the **Institute of Biosciences, Letters and
Exact Sciences (IBILCE)** of **UNESP — Universidade Estadual Paulista**, located
in São José do Rio Preto, SP, Brazil.

Our work brings together natural language processing, computer vision,
information retrieval, and machine learning to build AI systems that understand
and reason over language and multimedia data.
