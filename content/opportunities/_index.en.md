## 🔥 Open Positions — RATIONAL Project (FAPESP / SNSF)

Funded positions for a **Postdoctoral Fellow (PD)** and **Technical Training Specialists (TT-4)** are currently available in the international research project **RATIONAL: Robust Augmented Retrieval for Natural Language Inference over Transformer-based Models**, approved under the bilateral international call by **FAPESP** (São Paulo Research Foundation, Brazil) and the **SNSF** (Swiss National Science Foundation, Switzerland).

The project is an international research partnership between the **Idiap Research Institute** (Switzerland) and **UNESP** (Universidade Estadual Paulista, Rio Claro campus, Brazil), with research teams in both countries, co-led by **Prof. Dr. Andre Freitas (Idiap)** and **Prof. Dr. Daniel C. G. Pedronette (UNESP)**.

> **Core Research Topic:**  
> Investigating and developing robust evidence-based methods, obtained via Information Retrieval, for natural language inference tasks supported by Large Language Models (LLMs).

### How to Apply for RATIONAL Positions

Interested candidates should send an application by email by **September 20, 2026** to the UNESP Project Coordinator, **Prof. Dr. Daniel C. G. Pedronette**:

📧 **[daniel.pedronette@unesp.br](mailto:daniel.pedronette@unesp.br)**  
Subject line:
- For Technical Training: tag **`[RATIONAL-TT]`**
- For Postdoctoral Fellowship: tag **`[RATIONAL-PD]`**

Please include:
1. **Motivation letter** (max. 1 page) highlighting your background, relevant experience, and research interest in Information Retrieval, LLMs, and NLP;
2. **Updated Curriculum Vitae** (including links to Google Scholar, ORCID, or Lattes if applicable);
3. **Academic transcripts** (undergraduate and graduate);
4. Contact information for up to two academic or professional references (optional).

## Continuous Admissions & Who We Look For

In addition to specific funded grants, RISE-LAMD welcomes prospective students and researchers year-round:

- **Undergraduate Students (Scientific Initiation):** Curiosity and programming fundamentals (Python). We support applications for PIBIC and FAPESP fellowships.
- **MSc & PhD Students:** Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Applied Math, or related disciplines. Applications follow the respective UNESP graduate programs.
- **Postdoctoral Researchers & Collaborators:** Cross-institutional partnerships with labs and industry on generative AI, multimodal retrieval, and graph models.

For general inquiries, reach out to **[daniel.pedronette@unesp.br](mailto:daniel.pedronette@unesp.br)**.
