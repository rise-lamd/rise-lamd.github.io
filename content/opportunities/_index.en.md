We are always interested in motivated students and collaborators.

## Who we look for

- **Undergraduate students (IC):** curiosity and basic programming skills.
- **MSc / PhD students:** background in engineering, computer science,
  applied mathematics, or a related field.
- **Postdoctoral researchers:** experience in NLP, computer vision,
  information retrieval, or machine learning.
- **Collaborators:** researchers and industry partners with shared interests
  in language and multimedia AI.

## How to apply

Send an email to **{{< param "email" >}}** with:

1. A short message about your interests and why our group.
2. Your CV (and Lattes link, if you have one).
3. Your academic transcript (for student applications).

> We review applications continuously. Even when no funded position is
> open, we are happy to hear from strong, motivated candidates and to
> discuss scholarship opportunities (FAPESP, CNPq, CAPES).
