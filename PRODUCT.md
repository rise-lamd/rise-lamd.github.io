# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective undergraduate and graduate students, researchers, academic and
industry collaborators, peers looking for the group's publications, and current
RISE-LAMD members maintaining the site. Visitors need to understand the group's
research focus, people, outputs, and opportunities quickly. Maintainers need to
publish accurate bilingual content without becoming web developers.

## Product Purpose

RISE-LAMD is the bilingual public website of a UNESP research group working on
recognition, inference, and search over language and multimedia data. It makes
the group's identity, research, publications, people, news, and opportunities
easy to discover while keeping routine updates in plain YAML and Markdown. The
site succeeds when external visitors can evaluate the group's work and contact
the right people, and members can keep the information current with small,
reviewable edits.

## Brand Personality

Scientific, luminous, and confident. The site should feel like a precise
research instrument in which signals become structured knowledge. Its voice is
clear and technically credible without becoming institutional, promotional, or
opaque.

## Anti-references

- Generic academic templates whose identity comes from an upstream theme.
- Dense institutional portals that bury people and research under navigation.
- Generic AI-company landing pages with vague claims and decorative technology
  imagery that is unrelated to the group's work.
- Visual effects that reduce legibility, obscure evidence, or compete with the
  research artifacts.

## Design Principles

1. Show the research: use real people, diagrams, publications, and project
   artifacts as evidence of the group's work.
2. Turn signals into knowledge: structure content so relationships among data,
   methods, people, and outputs are easy to follow.
3. Keep content maintainable: routine updates belong in bilingual YAML and
   Markdown, not duplicated template markup.
4. Preserve bilingual parity: English and Portuguese are equal representations
   of the same group, not primary and secondary versions.
5. Prefer precise information over promotional language: names, venues, links,
   methods, and contributions should be concrete and verifiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA in both dark and light themes. Preserve keyboard navigation,
visible focus states, semantic headings, useful alternative text, 44 px touch
targets, sufficient color contrast, and a reduced-motion experience. Research
figures must include bilingual descriptions that communicate their purpose even
when the image cannot be seen.
